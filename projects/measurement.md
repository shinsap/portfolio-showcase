# Landing-page measurement: scope and evidence

My LP work includes GA4 and Google Search Console (GSC) setup and integration, alongside page structure, inquiry forms, and deployment. The goal is to connect search visibility with on-site behavior and inquiries.

| Area | Scope / available evidence |
|---|---|
| GSC | Search visibility and indexing; setup/integration are part of the stated work scope. This review did not inspect the live properties or verify every site's GA4–GSC product association |
| GA4 | Page and conversion-event design. BQmenu LP documents `cta_click` and `generate_lead` in addition to page views |
| Inquiry completion | BQmenu LP records both GA4-event verification and Web3Forms email receipt in production |
| Results | No quantified traffic, ranking, or conversion uplift is claimed from configuration alone |

For each site, distinguish implemented tracking, live account settings, and measured outcomes. Configuring both products does not by itself prove that their properties are linked, nor that acquisition improved.
