# Nortelia LP

## Overview

Nortelia LP is a landing page project intended to communicate a product or service clearly through a public-facing web experience.

It also includes the landing page for Hearing Aid Denchichou. That LP includes a hearing-aid battery search function and works as the lead-in to the Hearing Aid Denchichou PWA.

## Public URL

- LP: https://nortelia.co.jp

## Role

- Page structure planning
- Visual direction
- Frontend implementation
- Copy and content organization
- Product entry point design for Hearing Aid Denchichou
- Hearing-aid battery search LP flow
- Product showcase design for Nortelia-built apps and tools
- Deployment-oriented build checks

## Recent Update

A new **Built by Nortelia** product showcase was added near the top of the LP to make Nortelia's in-house product development visible at a glance.

- Horizontally scrolling product cards with actual product screenshots
- Cards for RepoBD, BQmenu, Rescue Pet Card, Gaichu-mae Navi, and Portion Weight Match
- Clickable cards that open each product in a new tab
- Desktop auto-scroll with drag support
- Mobile horizontal swipe support
- Pause/resume and reduced-motion accessibility handling
- Link from the showcase to the full **WEB APPS** section on the same page

The existing WEB APPS section remains the detailed product list, while the new card rail works as a visual showcase near the top of the page.
