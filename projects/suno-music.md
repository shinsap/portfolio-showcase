# Earlier Suno music-production experiment

An earlier content-production project combining Suno-generated music with AI-generated thumbnail images. Tracks were published on [YouTube: mainichi_bgm](https://www.youtube.com/@mainichi_bgm) and distributed through [DistroKid](https://distrokid.com/).

My role covered the content concept, music/image selection, publication, and distribution setup. This illustrates experience with generative-AI production and release workflows; it is separate from the engineering case studies. No custom music-generation API, automated distribution system, or measured commercial success is claimed. No source repository is presented.
